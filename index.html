Profile

About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT), specialising in offshore engineering and floating structures designs as well as hydrodynamics and hydroelasticity. Previously, from 2014 to 2016, I held a prestigious position as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Institute of Energy System (IES), University of Edinburgh. During this tenure, I spearheaded the hydrodynamic modeling efforts within the EPSRC EcoWatt2050 Project, showcasing my expertise and leadership skills. Prior to my role at IES, I garnered extensive industry experience, notably as a Senior Research Engineer at the Keppel Offshore and Marine Technology Centre. Additionally, I served as a Research Fellow at the esteemed National University of Singapore (NUS). My academic journey culminated in the attainment of my Ph.D. from NUS, with a specialization in the hydroelastic response of very large floating structures. My research endeavors focus on hydroelastic and hydrodynamic analysis, with particular interests in offshore engineering, coastal protection and flood management, marine engineering, marine digitalisation and AI, renewable energy, ocean space utilization, and the dynamics of very large floating structures.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Research at the intersection of offshore engineering, coastal resilience and sustainable marine technologies.

Floating Infrastructure

Very large floating structures, hydroelasticity, floating photovoltaics and modular floating platforms.

Marine Renewable Energy

Wave energy converters and integrated floating offshore renewable energy systems.

Numerical Modelling

Coupled fluid–structure interaction, hydrodynamic analysis and computational engineering.

Coastal Protection & Resilience

Wave attenuation, flood protection, floating breakwaters and nature-based coastal solutions.

Additive Manufacturing

Design, structural performance and manufacturing of marine components and vessels.

Smart Maritime Engineering

Digital twins, machine learning and vessel energy efficiency.
